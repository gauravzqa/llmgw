## What this shows

Across every successful streamed call, median TTFT was 604 ms direct and 629 ms through the gateway; median total 775 ms vs 779 ms. The per-scenario **+TTFT** column is the number to quote, read against the provider variance table: the gateway's cost is a few milliseconds of proxying plus its own first connection to the provider, and the provider's own run-to-run spread is tens to hundreds of milliseconds.

## Caveats

- Client and gateway share ONE shared-cpu-1x vCPU on the same machine (the gateway binds IPv4 only, so the sibling machine could not reach it over Fly's IPv6 private network; that is a deployment finding, not a benchmark choice). The load generator is light (<= 4 streams) but it is not free.
- N is small per scenario (4-8, 24 for multi-turn); p95s are indicative only.
- Provider latency dominates and varies by the minute; interleaving makes the paired delta fair, not the absolute numbers repeatable.
- The gateway arm is loopback; a real caller adds one in-region hop (~0.2-1 ms).
- `temperature=0` keeps the two arms' outputs near-identical but providers do not guarantee it; output-token counts are medians for that reason.
- Cold-start rows are one sample each.
